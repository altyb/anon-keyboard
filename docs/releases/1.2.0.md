**Install or update:** download `hudhud-1.2.0.apk` below. Updates in place. Android 8.0+, every phone.

**What's new**
- French, Spanish, German, Portuguese and Russian. Pick your languages in Settings
- Every Unicode emoji your phone can draw, with skin tones
- Undo, redo, select all, cut, copy, paste and a Voice button in the clipboard panel
- Voice hands over to a voice keyboard you installed, such as FUTO Voice Input
- Save and restore learned words and settings
- Light theme and accent colours
- Split layout on tablets and wide screens
- Copied images in clipboard history, where the app you type in accepts them
- Open source licenses screen and a privacy policy
