**New name: Hudhud Keyboard.** Same app: it updates in place and keeps your words and settings.

**Install or update:** download `hudhud-1.1.0.apk` below. Android 8.0+, every phone.

**What's new**
- Glide typing in English and Arabic, on the phone, no Google library. New and still being tuned; turn it off under Typing if it gets in your way
- Hold space and drag to move the cursor. A quick flick still switches language
- Slide on delete to remove whole words
- One-handed mode: long-press the emoji key
- Emoji search by English or Arabic name
- New name, new icon
