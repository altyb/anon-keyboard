First public release.

**Install:** download `anon-keyboard-1.0.0.apk` below. One APK for every Android phone, Android 8.0 or newer (all CPU types). Then open the app, tap **Enable keyboard**, and **Choose keyboard**.

**What's in it**
- English and Arabic, swipe the space bar to switch
- Word completion, next-word prediction, careful autocorrect with backspace undo
- Classic layout with symbols on the keys and long-press accents; Material You look as an option
- Clipboard with pins and a paste chip, emoji panel
- No internet permission. Password fields: no learning, no clipboard, hidden from screenshots
- Incognito switch, clipboard wiped on screen lock, clear-data buttons
- Settings in English and Arabic
