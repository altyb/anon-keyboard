A small Android keyboard for English and Arabic. It has no internet permission, so nothing you type can leave the phone.

- Glide typing in both languages, offline
- Word suggestions, next-word prediction and careful autocorrect that learn on the phone only
- Hold space and drag to move the cursor; slide on delete to remove whole words
- One-handed mode
- Clipboard history with pins
- Emoji panel with search by English or Arabic name
- Classic tall keys, or Material You colours
- Password fields: no learning, no history, hidden from screenshots
