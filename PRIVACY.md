# Privacy policy: Hudhud Keyboard

Last updated: 2026-10-31

Hudhud Keyboard does not collect, send or share anything you type. The app has no `INTERNET` permission, no accounts, no ads and no analytics, so it has no way to send data off the phone.

**What stays on your phone.**
- Words you type are learned so suggestions improve. You can turn this off or erase them in Settings.
- Clipboard history is kept in memory for a limited time and wiped when the screen locks, unless you change that. Only clips you pin are saved to storage.
- Recent emoji and your settings.

**What is never learned.** Nothing in password fields, in fields that ask for no learning, or while the incognito switch is on. Text with digits, `@`, `/` or `:` is not learned anywhere.

**Permissions.**
- Contacts (optional, off by default): names from your contacts are suggested while you type. They stay on the phone.

**Voice.** The Voice button hands over to another voice keyboard you installed. This app never records audio; that keyboard's own privacy policy applies.

**Backups.** Android cloud backup and device transfer are off. If you save a backup from Settings, it goes to the place you choose. That file holds your learned words and settings. It is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere safe. Clipboard history and pinned clips are left out.

**Contact.** Open an issue at https://github.com/altyb/hudhud/issues
